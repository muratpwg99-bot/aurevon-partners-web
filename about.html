About Aurevon Partners

Focused markets.
Independent thinking.

We are a family-led Luxembourg investment firm pursuing opportunities in sectors where specialist knowledge, structural change and long-term relevance intersect.

01 · Who we are

Built to form
our own view.

Aurevon Partners S.à r.l.-SPF is an independent, family-led investment company established in Luxembourg.

We invest exclusively for our own account and follow a focused, research-led approach. Our structure gives us the freedom to assess opportunities with patience, develop conviction independently and remain selective across market cycles.

We do not manage third-party money or provide investment services to the public.

02 · Investment focus

Four sectors.
Specific interests.

We concentrate on areas where we can develop a clear perspective on the business model, the operating environment and the sources of durable value.

01

Financials

AIFMs, GP stakes and specialist investment firms.

We seek ownership positions in specialised asset managers, AIFMs and GP or management-company economics. We value differentiated investment capabilities, sound governance and operating platforms that can scale without diluting accountability.

  • AIFMs
  • GP stakes
  • Specialist asset managers
  • Fund infrastructure
02

Technology

Cybersecurity and financial technology.

Our technology focus is concentrated on cybersecurity and fintech businesses solving mission-critical problems. Products embedded in essential workflows, recurring revenue and defensible technical capabilities matter more to us than short-lived themes.

  • Cybersecurity
  • Identity and resilience
  • Fintech software
  • Financial infrastructure
03

Industrials

Printing, packaging and machinery lifecycle services.

Our industrial focus is the printing and packaging value chain. We seek machinery, distribution and service businesses where installed-base access, rapid response, aftermarket capability and engineering know-how create recurring relevance.

  • Printing and converting
  • Packaging machinery
  • Service and retrofit
  • Automation and controls
04

Gaming

PC-first studios, enduring IP and enabling technology.

Our gaming interest is centred on premium PC and desktop gaming. We seek studios, publishers and enabling-technology businesses with distinctive IP, committed player communities, disciplined production and credible global distribution.

  • PC and desktop studios
  • Enduring game IP
  • Publishing and distribution
  • Developer tools
