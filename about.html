About Aurevon Partners

Focused markets.
Independent thinking.

We are a family-led Luxembourg investment firm pursuing opportunities in sectors where specialist knowledge, structural change and long-term relevance intersect.

01 · Who we are

Built to form
our own view.

Aurevon Partners S.à r.l.-SPF is an independent, family-led investment company established in Luxembourg.

We invest exclusively for our own account and follow a focused, research-led approach. Our structure gives us the freedom to assess opportunities with patience, develop conviction independently and remain selective across market cycles.

We do not manage third-party money or provide investment services to the public.

02 · Investment focus

Three sectors.
Specific interests.

We concentrate on areas where we can develop a clear perspective on the business model, the operating environment and the sources of durable value.

01

Financials

Specialist managers and financial infrastructure.

We look for investment opportunities across the asset-management ecosystem, including independent asset managers, regulated AIFMs and businesses supporting the operation of investment funds and financial markets. We are particularly interested in specialist expertise, strong governance, scalable operating models and enduring client relevance.

  • Asset managers
  • AIFMs
  • Fund and market infrastructure
02

Technology

Fintech, cybersecurity and Agentic AI as a Service.

Within technology, our focus spans fintech, cybersecurity and agentic AI delivered as a service. We seek businesses modernising financial infrastructure, building mission-critical security capabilities and developing autonomous AI systems that solve clearly defined operational problems. Technical depth, defensibility and practical adoption matter more to us than short-lived themes.

  • Fintech
  • Cybersecurity
  • AI infrastructure
  • Agentic AI as a Service
03

Industrials

Machinery, engineering and industrial know-how.

In industrials, we look for machinery manufacturers and engineering firms with proprietary know-how, high-quality products and meaningful positions in demanding end markets. We value technical capability, operational discipline and businesses whose expertise is difficult to replicate.

  • Machinery
  • Specialist engineering
  • Industrial technology
03 · Our lens

What earns
our attention.

We do not reduce every opportunity to a fixed template. Across sectors, however, the same qualities consistently shape our work.

01

Specialist edge

Distinct expertise, intellectual property or operating know-how that creates genuine differentiation.

02

Durable relevance

Products and services addressing important needs with the potential to remain valuable over time.

03

Quality of execution

Capable leadership, sound governance and the discipline to turn a strong position into lasting value.
