Every year, we support a select number of charitable projects, with a particular focus on children with disabilities and the people who care for them.

Annual philanthropic programme
01 · Our commitment

A yearly commitment
to giving back.

Aurevon Foundation is the philanthropic division of Aurevon Partners. Its central concern is helping children with disabilities access care, learning, participation and greater independence.

Each year, we select and participate in charitable projects that align with this commitment. Depending on the project, our contribution may combine financial support, time, expertise and access to our network.

02 · Areas of focus

Inclusion starts
with access.

01

Children with disabilities

Supporting practical initiatives that improve quality of life, participation and individual opportunity.

02

Inclusive education

Helping create learning environments, tools and pathways that recognise different needs and abilities.

03

Families & caregivers

Strengthening the people and organisations that provide essential care, guidance and everyday support.

04

Access & participation

Backing projects that reduce barriers to mobility, communication, culture, sport and community life.

03 · How we participate

Choose carefully.
Stay involved.

  1. 01

    Evidence before enthusiasm

    Understand the problem, the organisation and the realistic path to impact.

  2. 02

    Partnership over publicity

    Work with capable operators and prioritise useful support over performative visibility.

  3. 03

    Accountability over anecdotes

    Define goals early and review progress with proportional, practical measurement.

04 · Projects and enquiries

Start a
conversation.

Project introductions and general enquiries are handled directly by the Aurevon Foundation team.

Organisations working in our areas of focus may contact us by email. We review selected introductions as part of our annual planning; an enquiry does not create a commitment to provide support.
