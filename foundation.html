Aurevon Foundation Initiative

Purpose deserves
patience, too.

We are developing a focused philanthropic platform designed to bring the same long-term thinking to carefully selected non-profit projects.

Initiative in development
01 · Intent

From stewardship
to contribution.

The Aurevon Foundation Initiative is intended to become a dedicated expression of the family’s commitment to thoughtful, measurable and enduring social contribution.

The initiative is being designed separately from the SPF’s private wealth-management mandate. Its final legal form, governance and funding framework will be established before any grants or partnerships are undertaken.

02 · Intended focus

Four fields.
One long view.

01

Education

Expanding access to learning, practical skills and pathways that improve long-term opportunity.

02

Scientific progress

Supporting research and applied ideas with the potential to create meaningful public benefit.

03

Social mobility

Backing initiatives that help individuals and families build agency, capability and economic resilience.

04

Resilient communities

Contributing to local projects that strengthen social cohesion and durable community infrastructure.

03 · Intended method

Selective support.
Visible outcomes.

  1. 01

    Evidence before enthusiasm

    Understand the problem, the organisation and the realistic path to impact.

  2. 02

    Partnership over publicity

    Work with capable operators and prioritise useful support over performative visibility.

  3. 03

    Accountability over anecdotes

    Define goals early and review progress with proportional, practical measurement.

Important status note

An initiative being formed.

“Aurevon Foundation Initiative” currently describes a planned philanthropic programme. It is not presented as an incorporated or registered foundation, is not yet an active grant-making body and is not accepting unsolicited funding applications. Further information will be published once its legal structure and governance have been finalised.
