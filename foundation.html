Annual philanthropic programme
01 · Our commitment

A yearly commitment
to giving back.

Aurevon Foundation is the philanthropic division of Aurevon Partners and a dedicated expression of the family’s commitment to meaningful social contribution.

Each year, we select and participate in charitable projects that align with our values. Depending on the project, our contribution may combine financial support, time, expertise and access to our network.

02 · Areas of focus

Four fields.
One long view.

01

Education

Expanding access to learning, practical skills and pathways that improve long-term opportunity.

02

Scientific progress

Supporting research and applied ideas with the potential to create meaningful public benefit.

03

Social mobility

Backing initiatives that help individuals and families build agency, capability and economic resilience.

04

Resilient communities

Contributing to local projects that strengthen social cohesion and durable community infrastructure.

03 · How we participate

Choose carefully.
Stay involved.

  1. 01

    Evidence before enthusiasm

    Understand the problem, the organisation and the realistic path to impact.

  2. 02

    Partnership over publicity

    Work with capable operators and prioritise useful support over performative visibility.

  3. 03

    Accountability over anecdotes

    Define goals early and review progress with proportional, practical measurement.
