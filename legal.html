Nature of the company

Aurevon Partners S.à r.l.-SPF is a Luxembourg family wealth management company governed by the amended Luxembourg law of 11 May 2007 relating to the creation of a société de gestion de patrimoine familial (SPF). Its exclusive object is the acquisition, holding, management and realisation of eligible financial assets for private wealth purposes, excluding commercial activity.
