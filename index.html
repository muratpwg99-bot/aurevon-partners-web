01 · About us

A family-led firm.
Independent by design.

Aurevon Partners S.à r.l.-SPF is a family-led Luxembourg investment company. We invest for our own account across finance, technology, industrials and gaming.

Our work is shaped by curiosity, discipline and the freedom to form our own view. We look for specialist businesses in financials, technology, industrials and gaming where patient analysis can create an edge. Aurevon does not manage third-party money or offer investment services to the public.
