01 · About us

A family-led firm.
Independent by design.

Aurevon Partners S.à r.l.-SPF is a family-led Luxembourg investment company. We invest for our own account across finance, technology and industrials.

Our work is shaped by curiosity, discipline and the freedom to form our own view. We look for well-positioned businesses, durable ideas and situations where patient analysis creates an edge. Aurevon does not manage third-party money or offer investment services to the public.
